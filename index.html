How it works

Finish a real capstone project and it is assessed, scored and recorded on your Skill Passport. Proof of work beats a printed certificate every time.

Your verified profile feeds straight into freelance project matching and our hiring partners — or into your own client pipeline if you would rather work for yourself.

Three earning paths

A skill is only worth what you can do with it

Job boards only serve one of these. Freelance marketplaces only serve another. We think you should be able to choose.

For people who want stability, a team and a title.

  • Interview-focused capstone projects

Freelance

For people who want flexibility and multiple clients.

  • Portfolio built from real project work

Common questions

What exactly is Jobjila?

Jobjila is a skill-to-income platform. You learn a trending IT or professional skill from a practising trainer, prove that you can actually do it through a verified capstone project, and then use that verified profile to get a job, win freelance clients, or start your own service business.

How is this different from a normal online course platform?

Most platforms end at the certificate. Jobjila is built around what happens next — your verified Skill Passport feeds directly into freelance project matching and into our hiring partners. Training, freelancing and placement are one connected pipeline rather than three separate products.

Do you only teach IT courses?

No. IT, cloud and AI are where we started because demand is highest, but the platform is built for any skill that leads to income — marketing, design, data, business consulting and career skills, with more categories opening as trainers join.

What does a course cost?

Between ₹6,500 and ₹21,000 depending on length and cohort size. Every course page states its exact fee, duration and expected earning range up front. EMI options are available on courses above ₹10,000.

Can I earn while I am still learning?

That is the intention. Freelance-friendly courses such as digital marketing and web design put deliverable skills in your hands by around week five, and the Freelancing Bootcamp teaches you how to find and price your first client while you are still in a cohort.

Who teaches on Jobjila?

Practising professionals, not full-time lecturers — people currently working in the field they teach. Every trainer passes a recorded demo review before their first cohort, and their rating stays visible on their profile afterwards.

Not sure which skill fits you?

Tell us your background and what you want to earn. We will recommend a track honestly — including telling you when now is not the right time to spend money on a course.
