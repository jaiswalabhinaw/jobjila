Questions about our training

How much do the courses cost?

Between ₹12,000 and ₹21,000 depending on length and cohort size. Every course page states its exact fee. The first class is free, ₹500 holds your seat and is refundable, and the balance is due only before your third session.

Are classes live or pre-recorded?

Live online in the evening, taught by a practising consultant, with every session recorded so you can revisit it or catch up.

Are certification exam fees included?

No. Where a course prepares you for an external certification — AWS, Microsoft, Oracle, PeopleCert — you book and pay that exam directly with them. We cover the syllabus and run practice tests, but we never hold your exam money.

Can I take a course while working full time?

Most learners do. Courses run four to eight hours a week, sessions are in the evening, and everything is recorded. Tell us your schedule and we will suggest a batch.

Do you run training for company teams?

Yes. Closed batches on any subject here, scheduled around your working hours and quoted per batch rather than per seat. Message us with the team size and subject.

Do you guarantee a job afterwards?

No. We provide the training, an assessed capstone project, resume review and interview practice. We will not promise employment for a fee, and we would treat any institute that does with suspicion.

Not sure which course?

Send your background and what you want to be doing in a year. We will suggest a track — including if a shorter or cheaper one gets you there faster.
