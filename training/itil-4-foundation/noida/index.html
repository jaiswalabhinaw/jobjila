ITIL 4 hiring in Noida

Noida concentrates NCR's IT services and business process employment, particularly around Sectors 62 and 63 and along the Expressway corridor. It has a high volume of entry-level technology and support hiring and lower living costs than Gurgaon, which makes it a common first stop for people starting an IT career in North India.

ITIL appears in a large share of Noida service desk and delivery job descriptions — for many roles here it is treated as a baseline expectation rather than a bonus.

Pay: Noida entry-level salaries are moderate, but the volume of openings is high and living costs are lower than Gurgaon or Delhi, which makes the early-career maths work.
