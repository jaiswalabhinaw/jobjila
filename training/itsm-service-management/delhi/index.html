ITSM hiring in Delhi

Delhi's technology economy is less about large campuses and more about business services — consultancies, agencies, government-adjacent IT and a very large base of small and mid-size businesses. Nehru Place remains one of the country's biggest IT trading and services markets, and that density of businesses shapes what skills actually get paid for here.

Delhi's managed service providers and consultancies run service desks for clients across North India, and ITSM process skills are what those operations screen for.

Pay: Delhi salaries for equivalent roles sit close to the NCR average, with the strongest offers usually coming from Gurgaon and Noida employers that Delhi residents commute to.

ITSM in Delhi — questions

Where is IT work concentrated in Delhi?

Nehru Place remains the largest IT trading and services market, with business services clustered around Connaught Place, Okhla's industrial and back-office estate, and Netaji Subhash Place in the north-west. The pattern is many small employers rather than a few large campuses.

Is freelance IT work viable in Delhi?

More so than in most Indian cities, because the density of small and mid-size businesses creates steady demand for infrastructure setup, support and reporting work that is too small for a large firm to want. Several of our Delhi learners bill this alongside a job.
