Presales & Solution Consulting

The highest-paying pivot for technical people who can also talk to customers.

Book the free first class Syllabus

Presales sits between engineering and sales, and it is chronically short of good people. If you understand technology but are tired of being invisible to the business, this is the most direct route to a senior, well-paid, client-facing role — and almost nobody in India teaches it formally.

Syllabus

7 modules across 6 weeks, at about 5 hrs/week.

What presales really is

The deal cycle end to end, who does what, and the specific moments where a presales consultant wins or loses the deal.

Discovery that finds the real problem

Question frameworks, stakeholder mapping, and hearing the unstated constraint behind a stated requirement.

Solution design and architecture storytelling

Turning requirements into an architecture the customer's own team can defend internally after you leave.

The demo that closes

Structuring a demo around the customer's workflow, handling hostile questions, and recovering when something breaks live.

RFPs and proposal writing

Reading an RFP for hidden disqualifiers, writing compliant responses, and building a reusable answer library.

Pricing and effort estimation

Sizing a project, licence versus services margin, and defending your number when procurement pushes back.

Capstone: run a full deal cycle

Take a live scenario from discovery through solution, demo, proposal and pricing, assessed line by line.

What you will be able to do

  • Run a structured discovery call that surfaces the real requirement
  • Design and present a solution architecture to a mixed audience
  • Write a compliant, persuasive RFP response
  • Estimate effort and defend a price under pressure
  • Deliver a recorded demo good enough to use as a portfolio piece

Presales & Solution Consulting — questions

Who should take a presales course?

Developers, testers, support and infrastructure engineers who enjoy explaining technology to customers, plus salespeople who want to become technically credible. Some exposure to IT products or services is assumed.

Why is this the most expensive course on the site?

Cohorts are capped at 15 so that every learner gets individually reviewed demo and proposal practice with line-by-line feedback. That is the entire value of the course, and it does not scale to a large batch.

Is presales a sales job with a target?

Presales supports the sales team technically and usually carries a shared or team target rather than an individual quota. You are measured on solution quality, demo effectiveness and win rate, not on cold calling.

What salary can a presales consultant expect in India?

Moving into presales from a technical role commonly lands in the ₹8–12 LPA band, with experienced solution consultants well above that. These are market observations, not a promise from us.
