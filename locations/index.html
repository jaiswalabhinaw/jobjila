Questions about locations

Do you have physical centres in these cities?

No. Every Jobjila cohort runs live online, which is deliberate — it means the same trainer and the same curriculum wherever you live, no commute, and evening sessions that work alongside a job. The city pages exist because the job market and pay differ by city, not because the course does.

Are course fees different in different cities?

No. Fees are identical across India — there is no metro surcharge and no tier-2 discount. What varies by city is the salary you can expect afterwards and the kind of employers hiring, which is what each city page covers.

My city is not listed. Can I still join?

Yes, absolutely. Cohorts are online and open to anyone in India. These pages cover the cities most of our learners come from, but nothing stops you joining from anywhere else — message us on WhatsApp and we will get you into the next batch.

Not sure which course fits your city's job market?

Tell us where you are and what you want to earn. We will tell you what is realistically hiring near you — and what is not.
