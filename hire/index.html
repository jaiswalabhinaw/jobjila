Client questions

What does it cost to hire a freelancer through Jobjila?

Posting a project and receiving a shortlist is free. You agree a project value directly with the freelancer, and our commission is included in that figure — there is no separate platform fee added to your invoice.

How are freelancers verified?

Every profile carries a Skill Passport: assessed capstone projects, trainer endorsement, and ratings from previous work delivered through the platform. You are seeing evidence of completed work, not a self-reported skill list.

Do you also help with permanent hiring?

Yes. Our HR services cover permanent and contract recruitment, particularly for IT, infrastructure, marketing and support roles. Because many candidates are trained on our own platform, we can vouch for their skills first-hand.

What if the work delivered is not up to standard?

Milestone-based payment means you approve each stage before funds are released. If a milestone is not met, work continues until it is or the engagement ends without further payment. We step in directly if a dispute is not resolving.

How quickly can I get a shortlist?

For common skills — web development, digital marketing, cloud support, data reporting — expect a shortlist within two to three working days. Specialist requirements take longer, and we will tell you honestly if we cannot fill a brief.

Tell us what you need built or filled

Posting a brief is free and takes about two minutes. If we cannot fill it well, we will say so rather than sending you a weak shortlist.
