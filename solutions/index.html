Questions about solutions

Do you deliver this yourselves?

The assessment and solution design is ours. The delivery is done by a technology partner whose main business is that specific area — a cloud provider, a security firm, a networking specialist. We stay involved through proposal and delivery rather than handing you over and disappearing.

Why not go to the partner directly?

You can, and sometimes that is the right answer. What we add is the part before the sales conversation: working out what you actually need, what it should cost, and which provider suits your scale — from people who advise on this technology for a living rather than sell one product.

What does it cost me to ask?

Nothing. Sending us a requirement, the qualification call and the shortlist cost you nothing. You pay the partner for what they deliver, on terms you agree with them.

How long does it take?

A first response within two working days. How long the rest takes depends entirely on the scope — a hosting move is not a data-centre migration. We will tell you a realistic timeline rather than an encouraging one.

Deliver these services yourself?

If you are a cloud, hosting, security or managed-services provider, we bring qualified customer requirements to partners who can deliver them.
