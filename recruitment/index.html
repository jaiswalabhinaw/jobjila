Hiring

Hire IT people who have actually been assessed

Permanent, contract and freelance hiring for cloud, infrastructure, sales and presales roles. Our rate is published below, the employer pays it, and the candidate never pays us anything.

Send us a role Looking for a job instead?

The employer pays. The candidate never does.

This is the line between recruitment and the fee-harvesting that has given hiring agencies in India such a poor name. We are on the employer's side of the invoice, and we say so on every page of this site. If anyone asks a candidate for money in our name — a registration fee, a security deposit, a laptop charge — it is not us, and we would like to know about it.

What we recruit for

Four ways to engage, in the areas we actually know

We recruit in the technologies we advise on and teach. Outside those, we will tell you we are not the right agency rather than take the brief anyway.

Permanent recruitment

Full-time hires on your payroll. Invoiced after joining, at the published rate, with a 90-day replacement guarantee.

Contract hiring

Fixed-term people for a defined period — cover, a migration, a support ramp. Quoted per role with our margin disclosed.

Freelance and project-based hiring

Independent specialists for a scoped piece of work, drawn from our reviewed consultant network.

Specialist and niche roles

The hard-to-fill ones: OCI, ITSM tooling, cloud presales. Fewer candidates exist, so we tell you the realistic size of the pool up front.

Cloud and infrastructure roles

AWS, Azure and OCI engineers and architects, infrastructure and support staff. We teach these subjects, so we can screen on more than keywords.

Sales and presales roles

Solution consultants, presales and cloud sales. We run a presales course, which means we know what competence in this role looks like.

Startups and SMEs are welcome and are much of who we expect to work with. A single role is a perfectly normal engagement — there is no minimum.

How we work

Sourcing and screening, then a short shortlist

1. The brief

What the role actually needs, what it pays, and what you will compromise on. Free, and often the most useful hour.

2. Sourcing

Open market first, our network second. We tell you where a candidate came from every time.

3. Screening

A technical conversation with someone who does the work, not a keyword match. You get our written read, including the doubts.

4. Shortlist

Three or four people we would stand behind, not forty CVs. If we only have one, we send one.

When we put forward someone we trained, we tell you

We run training courses, and some of the people we place have taken them. That gives us an obvious interest in placing them — so we declare it in the first line of the submission, every time. Weigh our opinion accordingly and interview them as hard as you would anyone else. What we will not do is quietly present a former student as though we found them on the open market.

What it costs

The rate is published, like every other price on this site

Nobody in Indian recruitment publishes their rate. We publish every course fee, so it would be strange to start hiding things here.

8.33% of first-year CTC

For a permanent placement — about one month's salary. Invoiced after the person joins, never before. No retainer, no exclusivity, no charge for a role we fail to fill.

90-day replacement

If a permanent hire leaves or is let go within 90 days, we replace them at no further fee. If we cannot replace them, we refund what you paid.

Contract and freelance

Quoted per role, with our margin on the rate stated in writing before you agree. You always know what the person is being paid and what we are taking.

What we do not charge for

Briefing calls, sourcing, screening, rejected shortlists, and anything at all charged to a candidate.

Hiring questions

Who pays you?

The employer, always. We are engaged and paid by the company that is hiring. We have never charged a candidate a fee for anything, and we never will — not for a job, an interview, a placement or a CV review.

What do you charge?

8.33% of the hire's first-year CTC for a permanent placement — roughly one month's salary. It is invoiced only after the person joins. Contract and freelance engagements are quoted per role, with our margin disclosed in writing before you agree to anything.

What if the person leaves quickly?

If a permanent hire leaves or is let go within 90 days of joining, we find you a replacement at no further fee. If we cannot, we refund what you paid us. This is a replacement guarantee, not a placement guarantee — we do not promise that any given role will be filled.

Do you only put forward your own students?

No. We recruit from the open market, and most candidates we submit have no connection to our training. When we do put forward someone we trained, we say so in the first line of the submission, because we have an obvious interest in placing them.

How many people are on your bench?

For contract and freelance work we draw on our consultant network, which is small and reviewed rather than large and unvetted. We will tell you honestly how many people we have for your specific skill before you brief us, rather than after.

Have you done this before?

We are new to recruitment. We have been advising and training in these technologies for years, but our placement track record is short and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Weigh that when you decide how much of your hiring to give us.

Tell us about the role

Send the role, the location, the budget range and whether it is permanent or contract. We will tell you honestly whether we can fill it.
