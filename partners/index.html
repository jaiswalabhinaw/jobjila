What a partner can expect

Qualified, not cold

Every requirement has been through a scoping conversation before it reaches you — scale, timeline and decision process included.

Terms agreed in writing first

Lead registration, ownership and commercials are settled before any customer detail is shared. No verbal understandings.

Partner questions

What kind of opportunities do you bring?

Requirements from businesses that have come to us with a problem and been through our qualification call — so you receive a scoped need with a named contact, not a cold list.

What do you expect from a partner?

That you actually deliver in the area you claim, that you respond to a qualified requirement within a couple of days, and that you are straight with the customer about what you can and cannot do.

What are the commercial terms?

Agreed in writing with each partner before any customer information is shared, and they depend on the engagement — referral, co-sell or delivery-led. We do not publish a single rate because the models genuinely differ.

Do you work with small providers?

Yes. Fit matters more than size — a five-person specialist is often the right answer for a mid-size customer where a large integrator is not.
