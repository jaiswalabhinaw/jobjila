AI & Prompt Engineering for Professionals

Stop watching AI demos. Build the automations your workplace will pay for.

Enquire on WhatsApp See the syllabus

Most AI courses stop at clever prompts. This one goes further: you will connect an AI model to real documents, wire it into a workflow, measure whether the output is actually correct, and hand the result to someone who is not technical. That gap — between a demo and something a business can rely on — is where the money is.

Three ways this course pays you back

Get a job

AI Associate, Automation Analyst, AI-enabled Marketing or Ops roles

Freelance

Building AI assistants and document workflows for agencies and SMEs

Your own business

Selling AI automation retainers to local businesses

Syllabus

8 modules across 8 weeks, at roughly 5–6 hrs/week.

How language models actually work

Tokens, context windows, temperature and hallucination — enough mental model to debug bad output instead of guessing.

Prompt engineering that survives production

Structured prompts, few-shot examples, output formats, and why a prompt that works once may fail on the hundredth run.

Working with your own documents (RAG)

Chunking, embeddings and vector search — build an assistant that answers only from your company's PDFs and policies.

Automating real workflows

Connect AI to spreadsheets, email and forms using no-code tools, then graduate to simple API scripts.

Evaluating AI output

Build a test set, score responses, and prove to a client that your system is right often enough to trust.

Cost, privacy and safe deployment

Token economics, what you must never send to a public model, and how to talk to a client about data handling.

Packaging and pricing AI work

Scoping an automation project, writing the proposal, and setting a retainer that reflects saved hours.

Capstone: ship a working AI assistant

Deliver an end-to-end assistant for a real use case, with documentation and an evaluation report.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know coding to join this AI course?

No. The first half is entirely no-code and works through visual automation tools. Light Python appears in the second half, and we teach it from zero — you will be reading and editing scripts, not writing them from a blank file.

What are the AI and prompt engineering course fees?

₹14,500 for the full 8-week programme, including live sessions, recordings and API credits for your capstone project. EMI options are available.

Is this course useful if I am not from an IT background?

Very. Marketers, HR professionals, teachers and operations staff often get the fastest return, because they already know which repetitive task is worth automating. Around half of each cohort comes from non-IT roles.

Which AI models will I learn to work with?

The course is deliberately model-agnostic. You will work hands-on with current commercial models through their APIs and learn the patterns that transfer when models change — which they do, frequently.

Can I really earn freelance income from this?

Small businesses are actively paying for AI automation but struggle to find people who can scope it properly. The final module is dedicated to packaging, pricing and pitching, and your capstone doubles as your first portfolio piece.
