Presales & Solution Consulting

The highest-paying pivot for technical people who can also talk to customers.

Enrol now See the syllabus

Presales sits between engineering and sales, and it is chronically short of good people. If you understand technology but are tired of being invisible to the business, this is the most direct route to a senior, well-paid, client-facing role — and almost nobody in India teaches it formally.

Three ways this course pays you back

Get a job

Presales Consultant, Solution Architect, Technical Account Manager

Freelance

Bid and RFP support for IT services firms on a per-proposal basis

Your own business

Independent solution consulting for SaaS vendors entering new markets

Syllabus

8 modules across 6 weeks, at roughly 5 hrs/week.

What presales really is

The deal cycle end to end, who does what, and the specific moments where a presales consultant wins or loses the deal.

Discovery that uncovers the real problem

Question frameworks, stakeholder mapping, and hearing the unstated constraint behind a customer's stated requirement.

Solution design & architecture storytelling

Turning requirements into an architecture the customer's own team can defend internally after you leave the room.

The demo that closes

Structuring a demo around the customer's workflow, handling hostile questions, and recovering when something breaks live.

RFPs, RFIs and proposal writing

Reading an RFP for hidden disqualifiers, writing compliant responses, and building a reusable answer library.

Pricing, effort estimation & commercials

Sizing a project, licence versus services margin, and defending your number when procurement pushes back.

Objection handling & competitive positioning

Battlecards, landmines, and positioning against a competitor without ever naming them badly.

Capstone: run a full deal cycle

Take a live scenario from discovery through solution, demo, proposal and pricing — assessed by a working presales leader.

What you will be able to do

  • Run a structured discovery call that surfaces the real requirement
  • Design and present a solution architecture to a mixed audience
  • Write a compliant, persuasive RFP response
  • Estimate effort and defend a price under pressure
  • Deliver a recorded demo good enough to use as a portfolio piece

Frequently asked questions

Who should take a presales course?

Developers, testers, support engineers and infrastructure staff who enjoy explaining technology to customers, plus sales professionals who want to become technically credible. Some exposure to IT products or services is assumed.

What are the presales course fees?

₹21,000 for 6 weeks. It is our highest-priced course because every cohort is capped at 15 learners so that each person gets individually reviewed demo and proposal practice.

What salary can a presales consultant expect in India?

Entry into presales from a technical role commonly lands in the ₹8–12 LPA band, with experienced solution consultants and architects well above that. The role also carries incentive components tied to deal closure.

Is presales a sales job with a target?

Presales supports the sales team technically and usually carries a shared or team target rather than an individual quota. You are measured on solution quality, demo effectiveness and win rate, not on cold calling.

Will I get to practise demos with real feedback?

Yes — that is the core of the course. You deliver multiple recorded demos and receive line-by-line feedback from a practising presales leader, which most people have never had in their career.
