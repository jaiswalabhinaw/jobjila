The Presales & Solution Consulting job market in Lucknow

Lucknow's technology sector is younger than the established hubs, centred on Gomti Nagar and the Vibhuti Khand business district, with state IT-park development steadily adding capacity. For most people here the practical picture is a mix of a growing local market, a large SME base needing digital services, and remote work for employers and clients elsewhere.

Lucknow employers in Gomti Nagar, Vibhuti Khand, Amar Shaheed Path hire across SMEs, government-adjacent IT, education businesses and a growing remote workforce, and presales & solution consulting skills apply directly to that work.

Pay: Local salaries are below the major hubs, but Lucknow's living costs are low and the SME base makes independent client work realistic alongside or instead of a salaried role. For this skill specifically, the national band is ₹8–20 LPA as a Presales / Solution Consultant.
