The Website Design & Development job market in Lucknow

Lucknow's technology sector is younger than the established hubs, centred on Gomti Nagar and the Vibhuti Khand business district, with state IT-park development steadily adding capacity. For most people here the practical picture is a mix of a growing local market, a large SME base needing digital services, and remote work for employers and clients elsewhere.

Lucknow's SMEs, clinics, schools and retailers increasingly need websites, and very few local providers deliver them well — the bar to becoming the better option is low.

Pay: Local salaries are below the major hubs, but Lucknow's living costs are low and the SME base makes independent client work realistic alongside or instead of a salaried role. For this skill specifically, the national band is ₹15k–₹60k per website project.
