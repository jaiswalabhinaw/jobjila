Run real campaigns with a real budget — not screenshots of someone else's dashboard.

Digital marketing is the single most reliable skill on this platform for reaching all three earning paths — a job, freelance clients, or promoting your own business. This course is built around one rule: you never learn a channel without running something live in it.

Three ways this course pays you back

Get a job

Marketing your own venture without hiring an agency

Syllabus

8 modules across 10 weeks, at roughly 6 hrs/week.

Marketing fundamentals & positioning

Audience, offer and message — why most campaigns fail before a single rupee is spent on ads.

Search engine optimisation

Keyword research, on-page structure, technical SEO, local SEO and link building. You optimise a real site and track its movement.

Google Ads

Search, Performance Max and remarketing. Campaign structure, quality score, and reading the search-terms report like an operator.

Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

Audience building, creative testing, the pixel and conversions API, and diagnosing a campaign that stopped working.

Content & organic social

Content calendars, short-form video, and building a channel that compounds instead of needing constant spend.

Analytics & attribution

GA4, conversion tracking, UTM discipline, and building a dashboard a client can actually read.

Email, WhatsApp & retention

List building, automated sequences, and the retention maths that makes acquisition affordable.

Capstone: a live client campaign

Work on a real small business with a real budget, present results, and keep the case study for your portfolio.

  • Run profitable Google and Meta campaigns end to end
  • Build a GA4 dashboard that reports on business outcomes
  • Produce a client-ready monthly marketing report

Frequently asked questions

What are the digital marketing course fees at Jobjila?

₹15,500 for the 10-week programme. This includes live sessions, recordings, tool access during the course, and a small managed ad budget for your live capstone campaign so you are not spending your own money to learn.

Can I get a job after a digital marketing course?

Yes, this is one of the most active hiring categories for entry-level roles. What separates candidates who get hired is a portfolio of real campaigns with real numbers — which is exactly what the capstone produces.

Is digital marketing good for freelancing?

It is arguably the best freelance skill on this platform, because small businesses need it continuously and pay monthly retainers rather than one-off fees. Many learners start with one local client while still employed.

Do I need a marketing degree or background?

No. The course starts from fundamentals. Curiosity about why people buy things matters far more than any prior qualification.

Will I get certified in Google Ads?

We prepare you for the free Google Ads and GA4 certifications and run practice sessions, but those certificates are issued by Google directly. Your Jobjila Skill Passport separately records your verified campaign results, which employers find more convincing.
