The Digital Marketing & Growth job market in Chennai

Chennai's technology employment runs almost entirely along the Old Mahabalipuram Road corridor, from Tidel Park through Perungudi and out to Siruseri. The city has an unusually strong banking, financial services and insurance technology base, alongside manufacturing IT, which shapes what employers here screen for.

Chennai employers in OMR IT Corridor, Tidel Park, Siruseri SIPCOT hire across BFSI technology teams, IT services firms and manufacturing IT, and digital marketing & growth skills apply directly to that work.

Pay: Chennai salaries are broadly in line with Pune for equivalent roles, and living costs outside the OMR corridor remain among the more affordable of the major tech cities. For this skill specifically, the national band is ₹18k–₹45k/month, job or freelance.

Digital Marketing & Growth in Chennai — questions

Are sessions available in Tamil?

Courses are currently delivered in English, with trainers who are used to mixed-language cohorts and happy to clarify in simpler terms. Doubt threads are written, which many learners find easier than following a live explanation.

Do employers along OMR hire without a formal degree?

BFSI employers in Chennai tend to be more credential-conscious than startups elsewhere, so a degree still helps. What the capstone project does is get you past the initial screen and give you something substantive to discuss in interview.

What are the Digital Marketing & Growth course fees in Chennai?

The fee is ₹15,500 for the full 10 weeks programme, and it is the same wherever in India you join from — there is no separate Chennai pricing. That covers all live sessions, recordings and your capstone assessment. EMI options are available.

Is this Digital Marketing & Growth course online or classroom-based in Chennai?
