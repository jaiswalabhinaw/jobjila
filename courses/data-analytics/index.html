Data Analytics with Excel & Power BI

Turn spreadsheets into decisions — the fastest analytics route for non-coders.

Enrol now See the syllabus

You do not need Python to start a career in data. Most analyst work in Indian companies still happens in Excel, SQL and Power BI — and being genuinely good at those three, plus being able to explain what the numbers mean, is a complete and hireable skill set.

Freelance

Syllabus

8 modules across 8 weeks, at roughly 5 hrs/week.

Advanced Excel for analysts

Lookup functions, pivot tables, Power Query and dynamic arrays — cleaning messy real-world data without dread.

Data cleaning & preparation

Handling duplicates, missing values and inconsistent formats, and documenting what you changed and why.

SQL fundamentals

SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY and subqueries — enough SQL to pull your own data instead of waiting for someone else.

Power BI data modelling

Relationships, star schema and DAX measures that stay correct when filters change.

Dashboard design

Choosing the right chart, removing decoration, and building a dashboard an executive can read in ten seconds.

Statistics for business decisions

Averages that mislead, distributions, correlation versus causation, and sizing a difference that actually matters.

Storytelling with data

Structuring an analysis as a narrative and presenting a finding people will act on.

Capstone: a full analytics project

Take a raw messy dataset through cleaning, modelling and dashboarding to a presented recommendation.

What you will be able to do

  • Clean and reshape messy data reliably in Power Query
  • Write SQL to answer your own business questions
  • Build an interactive Power BI dashboard with correct DAX
  • Present an analysis that leads to a decision
  • Publish a full analytics case study to your Skill Passport

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Python for a data analyst job?

Not for most entry-level analyst roles in India, which run on Excel, SQL and a BI tool. Python becomes valuable later, particularly if you move towards data science — but it is not the barrier people assume it is.

What are the data analytics course fees?

₹13,500 for 8 weeks, including live sessions, recordings and all practice datasets. Power BI Desktop itself is free.

Is this course suitable for a non-IT background?

Yes, and commerce, finance and operations backgrounds often do particularly well because they already understand the business context behind the numbers.

What kind of job can I get after this course?

The common entry titles are Data Analyst, MIS Executive and Business Analyst. Reporting-heavy roles in finance, operations and marketing also hire directly from this skill set.

Will I work with real datasets?

Yes. Every module uses realistic, deliberately messy datasets rather than pre-cleaned textbook files, because cleaning is most of the actual job.
