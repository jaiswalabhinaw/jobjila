Frequently asked questions

Do I need a skill already to join the freelancing bootcamp?

Yes — this course teaches the business of freelancing, not a delivery skill. It works best taken alongside or straight after a skill course such as digital marketing, web design or AI automation.

What are the freelancing bootcamp fees?

₹6,500 for 4 weeks. It is intentionally our most affordable course because it multiplies the value of every other course on the platform.

Will Jobjila give me clients directly?

Graduates with a verified Skill Passport are eligible to be matched with projects posted by clients on the platform. That is a genuine channel, but the bootcamp deliberately teaches you to build your own pipeline so you are never dependent on one source.

Can I freelance while working a full-time job?

Most learners in this bootcamp do exactly that. The workload is designed around evenings and weekends. Do check your employment contract for any moonlighting clause before you take paid work.

How much can a beginner freelancer realistically earn?

Early projects in the Indian market commonly range from ₹5,000 to ₹25,000 depending on the skill. What changes the picture is retainers — one ₹20,000 monthly retainer is worth more than chasing five one-off jobs.
