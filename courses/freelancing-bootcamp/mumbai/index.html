The Freelancing Bootcamp job market in Mumbai

Mumbai's technology jobs sit alongside finance and media rather than in a standalone tech corridor. Banking technology clusters around BKC, product and startup work around Powai, and a very large advertising and media industry across Andheri and Lower Parel — which makes Mumbai the strongest city in India for marketing and content skills specifically.

The density of agencies and small businesses across Andheri and Lower Parel makes Mumbai one of the easiest cities in India to find your first paying freelance client.
