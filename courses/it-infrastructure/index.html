IT Infrastructure & Networking

The layer everything else runs on — and a reliable entry door into IT.

Enquire on WhatsApp See the syllabus

Every company that owns a laptop needs someone who understands infrastructure. This course builds that foundation properly — networking, servers, identity and virtualisation — and it remains one of the most dependable ways into an IT career without a computer-science degree.

Three ways this course pays you back

Get a job

Freelance

Your own business

Annual maintenance contracts for local businesses

Syllabus

8 modules across 10 weeks, at roughly 6 hrs/week.

How networks actually move data

The OSI model made practical: IP addressing, subnetting, DNS and DHCP, with packet captures you can read.

Routing and switching

VLANs, static and dynamic routing, and building a small multi-site network in a simulator.

Server administration

Windows Server and Linux side by side — users, permissions, file shares, scheduled jobs and patching.

Active Directory & identity

Domains, group policy and single sign-on — the backbone of nearly every corporate IT estate.

Virtualisation

Hypervisors, virtual machines, snapshots and resource planning using VMware and Hyper-V.

Backup, recovery & business continuity

Backup strategies, RTO and RPO, and actually restoring from a backup under time pressure.

Security hardening & monitoring

Firewalls, VPNs, endpoint protection and reading logs to spot a problem before users report it.

Capstone: design a small-office IT estate

Deliver a full network diagram, server plan, backup policy and cost sheet for a 40-person company.

What you will be able to do

  • Subnet a network correctly without a calculator
  • Build and manage a Windows domain with group policy
  • Design a backup and recovery plan you have personally tested
  • Diagnose connectivity faults methodically instead of by trial and error
  • Present a complete small-office infrastructure design

Frequently asked questions

Is this course enough to get an IT support job?

It covers what entry-level IT support and infrastructure roles test for: networking fundamentals, Windows and Linux administration, Active Directory and troubleshooting method. Combined with the capstone and mock interviews, learners typically target IT Support Engineer or Network Administrator roles.

What are the IT infrastructure course fees?

₹16,000 for 10 weeks, including live sessions, recorded lectures and remote lab access for the full duration of the course plus 30 days afterwards.

Does this prepare me for CCNA?

The routing, switching and networking modules cover a substantial part of the CCNA foundation. It is not a CCNA exam-cram course — the goal is working competence — but learners who go on to CCNA find the groundwork already done.

Do I need my own hardware or lab equipment?

No. All practical work is done in simulators and remote labs that we provide. A laptop with 8 GB of RAM and a stable internet connection is sufficient.

How is this different from the cloud computing course?

This course covers the traditional infrastructure most companies still run, and it is the better starting point if you are completely new to IT. Many learners take this first and follow it with the cloud course.
